Connecting to BuddyGPT
=======================

`BuddyGPT <https://ai.hpc.uco.edu/>`_ is an LLM service that provides UCO researchers 
with access to open source AI models such as LLaMA. It is hosted entirely on UCO's 
on-premises resources, ensuring that chats, documents, and models are not shared 
between users nor used for training.

There are two ways to interact with BuddyGPT:

* **UI** — A web-based chat interface accessible via your UCO credentials.
* **API** — Programmatic access for integrating BuddyGPT into your workflows.

.. note::
   Access is currently granted on a per-request basis. To request access, 
   reach out to hpc@uco.edu with the subject line "BuddyGPT Access Request".

For full details on setup, models, and usage, see :ref:`buddy-gpt`.
